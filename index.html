How to play. Open a game on the projector, set the number of teams (2–6) and rename them. Teams choose a category and a point value; click the tile to show the clue. Press Show answer (or the A key) to reveal it, use the + / − buttons to award or deduct the points, then Close. Answered tiles grey out. When the board is done, press Final Jeopardy: teams write a wager first, then reveal the clue, and type the wager into the points box before awarding. Scores are remembered by the browser until you press Reset game.

Spartan Society: Survey & Government works a little differently: it has six categories, a hidden Daily Double and a 30-second timer for Final Jeopardy. Press Show answer (or Space), then click the team that earns the points; Escape returns to the board. Its start screen explains the rest.

Difficulty. 100 points = core recall every student should know; 300 = a specific detail or source; 500 = a historian's view, a piece of evidence, or a link between ideas. No question is repeated across Sparta I and II, Greek World I and II, and Xerxes I and II, so the two games for a topic can be played on different days. Survey & Government is a focused review of two Sparta dot points, so it revisits some core facts from Sparta I.
