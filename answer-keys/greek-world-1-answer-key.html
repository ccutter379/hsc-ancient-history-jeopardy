Final Jeopardy — Reasons for Greek victory and Persian defeat
Reasons for Greek victory and Persian defeat
wagerHerodotus (9.62–63) names 'the chief cause' of the Persian defeat at Plataea. What was it — and name ONE 'structural' factor that modern historians such as Lazenby, Green or Holland stress instead of Herodotus' freedom-versus-slavery framing.Their lack of armour, 'fighting without it against heavily armed infantry'. Structural factors (any one): Greek use of terrain and bottlenecks; Persian logistics and the 'tyranny of distance' — the army depended on the fleet, so Salamis broke the combined-arms strategy; the hoplite-versus-light-infantry mismatch; Athenian naval tactics (ramming, the periplus, the kyklos); the storms that wrecked some 400 Persian ships; the mixed loyalties of the multi-ethnic Persian force

Answers marked "any two" / "any one" accept any of the listed alternatives. Point values rise with difficulty: 100 = core recall, 300 = a specific detail or source, 500 = interpretation, evidence or a historian's view.
