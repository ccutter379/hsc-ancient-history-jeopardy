Final Jeopardy — Evaluating the sources: Herodotus and Persepolis
One particular source or type of source: Herodotus Books VII–IX and Persepolis — value and limitations; problems of evidence
wagerGive ONE strength and ONE limitation of Herodotus as a source for Xerxes, and ONE strength and ONE limitation of Persepolis.Herodotus — strengths (any): the only continuous narrative of the invasion; close in time and drew on eyewitnesses; preserves Persian voices such as Demaratus and Artemisia. Limitations (any): Greek and hostile; shaped by the hubris–nemesis frame; reports private scenes he could not have witnessed. Persepolis — strengths (any): a contemporary Persian voice in the king's own words; reveals royal ideology; the tablets reveal the real economy. Limitations (any): pure ideology with no events or chronology; royal propaganda; never mentions the Greek defeat
